abstract: >-
  This paper examines how loan officer industry specialization shapes corporate
  loan pricing and contract design. Using hand-collected data linking individual
  officers to large corporate loans from 1995–2023, I show that more specialized
  officers at the same bank, lending to the same borrower, charge lower interest
  spreads after controlling for relationship lending and bank-level
  specialization. A Bartik-style instrument based on national industry credit
  growth mitigates endogeneity concerns. Pricing gains are concentrated among
  informationally opaque borrowers. Beyond pricing, specialized officers also
  write more flexible, customized contracts without altering borrowers' ex post
  performance. Overall, industry-specific expertise at the officer level
  ameliorates information frictions and improves contractual efficiency.
